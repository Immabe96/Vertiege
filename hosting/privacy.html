Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly: profile data, posts, chats, achievement proofs, and device tokens for notifications. If you use Campfire voice, we process audio for the live session only.

How we use your data

Data is used to provide and improve Vertiege, personalize your experience, verify achievements, and send important updates. We never sell your personal data.

Storage & security

Data is stored on Supabase with encryption in transit and at rest. You may delete your account from Settings; deletion removes your profile and associated personal data from our systems.
